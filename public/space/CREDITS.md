# The Earth

NASA's Visible Earth, public domain: the Blue Marble Next Generation (December 2004, with
topography and bathymetry; `world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg`, Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth
Observatory), re-encoded as WebP at 4096 × 2048, quality 85. The clouds are procedural
(`src/clouds`, from web-gpu-gems).
