# Sky HDRIs

The Qwantani series from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), CC0 (public domain), 1k Radiance
HDR, "puresky" versions (the sky alone): morning, noon, afternoon, late afternoon, sunset,
dusk 2 and night. `index.json` (the sun's direction, colour and irradiance in each, and the
sky's mean colour) is written by `scripts/hdri-sun.ts`.
